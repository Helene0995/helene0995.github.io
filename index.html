About

What I enjoy most is the moment a screen becomes useful: the page loads fast, the data is right, and the person using it knows exactly what to do. I like working on the whole path to get there, from the database and the API to the interface.

Solar Production Dashboard

Sylfen, France
2022 – 2024

A web dashboard that shows a client's solar production and compares it with what the weather forecast predicted.
